As I work through my retirement, I am going to want to track reality vs. predictions made in this app. So, I want a way to periodically update actuals that replace predictions. For example, the modelled monte-carlo simulation might predict market conditions that are not aligned with the actual market conditions for a given year. I would want to be able to hardcode, for example, that 2026 equities returned 3.5% vs. the modelled 12%. Same for fixed incomes, CPI, health care inflation and other values that make sense. I would also want a way to track my actual expenses vs. budgeted. Each year, the IRMAA tiers, tax brackets, medicare premiums, etc will also change but not necessarily based on exact inflaction adjustments. I would want a way to input the actuals year by year. All the input actuals should seamlessly replace simulated values for whatever period I enter them. For example, if I enter actuals for 2026 thru 2028, then those two years of simulated predicated values should be subsituted with the 2026 thru 2028 actuals I entered. These must be persisted and always used unless they are deleted or changed. 

Another interesting idea is one of a guardrail plan. This idea considers what my planned budgeted spending is for a year. It allows me to set and upper and lower guardrail value that is some percentage away from the overally budgeted yearly expenses. If the market does better than the model predicted leaving me a positive gap, or I spent less than expected also leaving me a positive gap and the total is still under my upper guardrail, then I am giving myself permission to spend even more due to the excess gap between my upper guardrail and actual values. The reverse happens when the gap is negative - I should plan on spending less to compensate.
